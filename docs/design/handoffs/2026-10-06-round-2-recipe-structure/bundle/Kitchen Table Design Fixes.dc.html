Answers to the brief, in sections A1–A4, B and C. Each row has the spec on the left and the screens on the right. Screens are 360dp wide. Use Tweaks to switch theme and to compare the timeline and note alternatives.
A1Headings and notes between steps
Heading

Uses the group label style: titleSmall (14/20, weight 600) in primary, the same green as the step discs, so it belongs to the timeline. It sits flush with the disc column, 32 below the previous step and 12 above the next one.

The connector breaks at a heading, so each group reads as its own run. The numbers stay continuous, so the method still reads as one sequence.

Note

Sits in the text column (44 from the left) with no disc and no number. Uses bodyMedium in onSurfaceVariant on a radius-8 card panel with 8/12 padding. The connector runs past it, so the sequence isn't interrupted. Warnings use the same quiet panel: the voice stays calm, never red.

Numbering continues: 1–4, then 5–7

Keep it continuous. "Step 5" is then one unambiguous place for cooking mode, for progress and for someone saying "I'm on 5". If numbering restarted, there would be two step 2s in the same recipe.

Editor

Every row keeps the same frame: a grip, a label row and a field. The label says what the row is: "Step 5", or an icon with "Heading" or "Note". Each label row ends with an ✕ that deletes the row. Deleting a heading keeps its steps and shows "Heading removed." with Undo.

At the end, one row adds a Step (tonal), a Heading or a Note. Pressing Enter in a step field still starts the next step. All three kinds drag by the grip. A dragged row lifts to level 3, and the drop line is 2dp primary.

A recipe with no headings and no notes (A1·2) uses the same rows and is pixel-identical to today's StepList.

A3Light text formatting
Rendering (bodyLarge 18/28)

Bold is weight 600, not 700, because 700 belongs to recipe names. Italic is the true italic. Both keep the text colour.

Bullet: a 6dp disc in onSurfaceVariant, centred on the first line. The glyph sits 4 in from the text column and the item text starts 20 in. Numbered: "1." in onSurfaceVariant with tabular figures, in the same 20 column.

4 between list items and 12 between paragraphs or blocks. Wrapped lines hang at the text indent. Inside a step, the list hangs from the step's text column, so the disc and timeline stay clear (A3·1, step 2).

bodyMedium contexts (description, step notes) use the same rules at 14/20.

Editing: a toolbar above the keyboard

A 48dp bar on surfaceContainer with a hairline on top. It appears only while the description, a step, a note or the end note is focused. It has four 48dp toggles (bold, italic, bullet list, numbered list) and a hide-keyboard button at the end. An active toggle gets the mustard secondaryContainer pill, the same as a selected chip.

Fields show formatting as you type (WYSIWYG), so cooks never see **. Typing "- " or "1. " at the start of a line also starts a list. A toolbar is the smallest thing that makes formatting discoverable without a menu.

A4Ingredient editing and review
Write it like a list, see it as a recipe

The editor shows each line as the parsed recipe row: name on the left, amount on the right, the note below, dashed 6/4 dividers. Only a grip is added. The page then reads like the recipe it will become, not a column of fields.

Tap a line to open it into one field showing the raw text the cook typed. Under the text, a live preview row shows the parse in the final layout: → flour · sifted   2–3 cups. Ranges and fractions appear as typed (2–3, ½).

Fast entry: Enter commits the line and opens a new empty one below it, and the keyboard stays up. Backspace on an empty line deletes it and moves up. The open line has an ✕ to delete it. Lines drag by the grip.

States

Matched: no marker. A clean row means OK. Suggestion: a 32dp mustard pill "cinnamon?" with a check under the row. Tapping the pill confirms it, and tapping the row lets the cook choose another. Unmatched: a 14dp dashed ring after the name, never red. Optional: "· optional" in bodyMedium. No amount: the right column is empty.

Flagged (review): a 3px reviewMarker inset edge on a surfaceContainerLow tint, with a 13 text inset.

Sections

Same as the step heading in A1: titleSmall in primary, 24 above and 4 below. In the editor it gets a grip and drags too, taking its lines with it. "Add section" sits next to "Add ingredient".

BShopping list: item markers
Quantities on the right, status after the name

More than one unit family: every amount goes in the amount column, stacked and right-aligned. The second and later amounts start with a muted "+", and each number is primary w600. In a shop you read down one column, so nothing hides in a trailer line under the name.

Unmatched: the raw line is the name, followed by the 16dp dashed ring. The muted trailer now says why: "Not added up · from Sauerkraut sarma".

Pantry staple: a 16dp outlined push_pin in outline after the name. It marks items that always go to Probably have. Items moved there once have no pin.

Discovering the long-press

The collapsed card's subline changes from "Cupboard staples" to "Long-press any item to move it here." It teaches the gesture right where the result lands. A long-press now opens a 2-item menu instead of moving the item silently. The menu offers "Move to Probably have" and "Always put here", and the second one sets the pin. The move confirms with a snackbar and Undo.

Shared row

The stacked amounts and the dashed ring belong to IngredientLineRow, so they also apply in recipe detail and import review. The pin, the long-press menu and the hint are list-only. No check-off state is added.

CMeal plan: full weekday names
Fits at 360dp

Headers read "Ponedeljak 5" / "Monday 5" in titleSmall. The inner width of a card at 360dp is 360 − 32 gutter − 20 padding = 308.

Worst expanded row: "Ponedeljak 5" (≈84) + 8 + "Danas" tag (≈56) = ≈148. That leaves plenty of room. Worst collapsed row: "Četvrtak 8" (≈70) + the drop hint "Pustite ovde" (≈80) + "Dodaj obrok" (≈128) = ≈294 of 308. It fits but it's tight (C·1, Thursday). If a longer translation ever lands, hide the drop hint's text while dragging, because the 2dp ring already says "drop here".

The leftover meta stays abbreviated: "Večera · od pon 5", "Dinner · from Mon 5".

Screen dates are illustrative: the week of 5–11 October, with Monday as today to test the longest name.
