D1Where the switcher lives
(a) Settings only · not chosen

The tabs stay exactly as they are today. In Settings, the Household row opens a list of your households. Why: no new chrome, and that suits how rarely people switch. But nothing on Recipes, Plan or List says which household you're in, so you can add to the wrong list without noticing.

(b) Household under the tab title · accepted

With two or more households, the Recipes, Plan and List headers get a second line with the current name and a ▾. Tapping the title block (56dp) opens a sheet to switch. Why: the name sits right where you add things, so it reminds you where you are without being a separate warning.

Why (b)

People switch a few times a week at most, so a one-tap switcher isn't the point. The point is the label. Adding to the wrong household is silent and costly. It's prevented by one line of bodyMedium that only people with 2+ households ever see. The title stays at the same x (16) and size. With one household, the header is the standard AppBar, identical to today (D1·1).

Management (rename, members, leave) stays in Settings for both directions, so (b) only adds a shortcut.

D2List of your households
Getting there

One household: the Settings row shows "Kod Mire · 2 members" and opens the Household screen, as today. Two or more: the group label becomes "Households". The row shows the current name and "2 members · 1 more household", and opens this list (D2·1–6).

Row switches, gear manages

Tapping the row switches to that household. The leading radio says it's a choice, and the current one is filled in primary. The gear is a separate 48dp button behind a 32dp hairline. It opens that household's Household screen, whether or not it's the current one. Phones use the same split for Wi-Fi networks, and the intro line under the title says it in words.

Meta: "N members · Owner / Member" (sr: člana / članova · Vlasnik / Član). Names wrap to two lines here and are never cut off. They're truncated only in the one-line header. The order stays fixed (the order you joined), so rows don't move when you switch.

Actions at the end

"Create a new household" and "Join with a code" are plain ListRows below the card, so they don't read as households.

The way in with one household

One addition to today's Household screen: a last section, "Another home?", with an outlined "Add a household" that opens this list (D2·7–8). It sits below everything that exists today.

D3Switching
1 · Sheet

Opens from the header line on Recipes, Plan or List. It uses the same HouseholdRow as D2, without the gear. Tapping a row switches and closes the sheet (250 ms). "Manage households" at the bottom opens the D2 list in Settings. The sheet uses the same surfaceContainerLow, radius 28 and level 3 as Add meal.

2 · Loading

The header shows the new name straight away, so the change registers before the data arrives. A 4dp linear progress bar in primary runs under the header. The tab keeps its frame (segments, nav). The content is replaced by flat placeholder bars in surfaceContainerHighest on the card. There's no shimmer, and the progress bar is the only motion. Controls that write are disabled until the data has loaded.

3 · Landed

You always land on the root of the current tab. Any open detail screens are popped. The snackbar reads "Now in Jovanovići." with Undo, which switches back, so a mis-tap costs one tap. Serbian: "Sada ste u domaćinstvu „…“." It wraps to two lines with the long name.

D4Create and join from inside the app
Same screens, three changes

1. An AppBar with ✕. It's a task you can abandon, and ✕ returns you to the list. 2. No 56dp mark, which belongs to the first-run welcome. The headlineSmall title moves up to 8 below the bar. 3. The title says "another": "Create another household" / "Napravite još jedno domaćinstvo". The long Serbian title wraps to two lines.

The body says the current household is untouched. The cross-link button ("I have an invite code" / "Create a household instead") is dropped, because the list already offers both. One primary button stays pinned to the bottom.

Where you land

In the new household, on the Recipes root. The header line appears for the first time here. Snackbar: "Now in Vikendica." After Create, the action is Invite, which opens that household's screen with the invite code. After Join, the action is Undo.

Already a member

If the code is for a household you're already in, this isn't an error, so it's not shown in red. A quiet card panel under the field reads "You're already a member of Jovanovići." The primary button becomes "Switch to Jovanovići", or "Done" if it's the current household. "Use another code" clears the field.

D5Leaving or deleting when you have others
The dialog names where you'll go

The title names the household, because you may have several. The body keeps today's explicit consequences, then ends with one sentence in onSurface: "You'll switch to Jovanovići." The household you switch to is the one you used most recently among the others.

Owner: "Delete household" replaces "Leave". The body is the handover's destructive copy plus the switch sentence (D5·3–4). The long Serbian name wraps in the title.

Leaving a household you're not in right now (reached through the gear): there's no switch sentence and you stay where you are.

Afterwards

You land on the Settings root in the next household, with the snackbar "You left Kod Mire. Now in Jovanovići." There's no Undo, because leaving can't be undone from here.

Your last household: the dialog copy is as today, without the switch sentence. Afterwards you go back to first-run onboarding (Name your household), as today.

D6Knowing where you are
Tabs: the header line is enough

On Recipes, Plan and List, the D1·b line is the reminder. The recipe editor, the Add meal sheet and list edits all open from a tab that shows the name, so they add nothing.

Import review: one line before Save

This is the one write that can start outside the app: you share a link from the browser and never see a tab. A single bodyMedium line sits above the pinned buttons, inside the existing hairline footer: home "Saves to Kod Mire" with a text button, Change.

Change opens the D3 sheet, titled "Save to". It chooses where this import is saved, and you don't switch households. The snackbar says "Saved to Jovanovići."

Nothing else

There's no colour per household, and no name on recipe detail, settings rows or the copied list. With one household, the import footer is identical to today: two buttons, no line.
